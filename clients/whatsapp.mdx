## Quick Start

Get started with Khoj on WhatsApp:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open WhatsApp">
    Launch WhatsApp on your phone.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Contact Khoj">
    Text [+1 (848) 800-4242](https://wa.me/18488004242) or scan the QR code above.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Start Chatting">
    Begin asking questions immediately. The bot works right away for generic queries!
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  The WhatsApp bot works immediately for general questions. To access your personal knowledge base, you'll need to connect it to your Khoj Cloud account.
</Info>

## Connect Your Account

To chat with your own notes and documents:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create Khoj Cloud Account">
    Sign up at [app.khoj.dev](https://app.khoj.dev) if you haven't already.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Upload Your Data">
    Use the [Desktop app](/clients/desktop) or [Obsidian plugin](/clients/obsidian) to sync your files with Khoj.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Connect WhatsApp">
    Go to the [Khoj Cloud Settings page](https://app.khoj.dev/settings) and follow the instructions to link your WhatsApp account.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Start Using Your Data">
    Once connected, the WhatsApp bot can access and search your personal knowledge base!
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  You need one of the desktop clients (Desktop app, Obsidian, or Emacs) to share and sync your data with Khoj before using it via WhatsApp.
</Note>

## Slash Commands

Use these commands to access specific features:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="/online - Online Search" icon="globe">
    Get responses from Khoj powered by real-time online search.
    
    **Example:**
    ```
    /online What's the latest news about AI?
    ```
    
    ```
    /online Current weather in San Francisco
    ```
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="/dream - Image Generation" icon="image">
    Generate an image based on your text description.
    
    **Example:**
    ```
    /dream A serene mountain landscape at sunset
    ```
    
    ```
    /dream Futuristic city with flying cars
    ```
    
    <Note>
      Image generation may have usage limits depending on your Khoj plan.
    </Note>
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="/notes - Search Your Notes" icon="book">
    Explicitly force Khoj to retrieve context from your personal notes.
    
    **Example:**
    ```
    /notes What did I write about project planning?
    ```
    
    ```
    /notes Meeting notes from last week
    ```
    
    <Warning>
      This command requires your WhatsApp account to be connected to a Khoj Cloud account with uploaded data.
    </Warning>
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Bot not responding">
    1. Verify you've sent a message to the correct number: +1 (848) 800-4242
    2. Check your internet connection
    3. Wait a moment - the bot might be processing your request
    4. Try sending a simple message like "hello"
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Cannot access my notes">
    This usually means:
    1. Your WhatsApp account isn't connected to Khoj Cloud
    2. You haven't uploaded any data to Khoj yet
    
    **Solution:**
    - Connect your account via [Khoj Settings](https://app.khoj.dev/settings)
    - Use the [Desktop app](/clients/desktop) or [Obsidian plugin](/clients/obsidian) to sync your data
    - Try the `/notes` command explicitly
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Usage limit reached">
    You've hit your daily quota. Options:
    1. Wait until tomorrow when limits reset
    2. [Upgrade to a paid plan](https://khoj.dev/#pricing)
    3. Use the [web client](/clients/web) or other clients for unlimited access
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Image generation not working">
    Possible reasons:
    1. Daily image generation limit reached
    2. Prompt violates content policy
    3. Temporary service issue
    
    Try:
    - Rephrasing your prompt
    - Checking your usage limits
    - Trying again later
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
