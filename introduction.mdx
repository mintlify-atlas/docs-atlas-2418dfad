## What Makes Khoj Different?

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Open Source & Self-Hostable">
    Khoj is fully open-source under AGPL-3.0 license. Run it privately on your computer or use our cloud service. Your data, your choice.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Works with Your Data">
    Supports images, PDFs, markdown, org-mode, Word docs, Notion pages, and GitHub repositories. Khoj understands your documents and uses them to provide contextual answers.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Bring Your Own LLM">
    Use any local or online LLM. Compatible with OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, or local models via Ollama, LiteLLM, and LM Studio.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Built for Privacy">
    Self-hosting ensures your data never leaves your machine. All search and indexing happens locally when you self-host.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
