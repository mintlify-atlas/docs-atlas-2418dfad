## Creating an Automation

<Steps>
  <Step title="Access Automations Page">
    Navigate to [app.khoj.dev/automations](https://app.khoj.dev/automations)
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Click 'New Automation'">
    Or use the quick-create form on the automations page
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Configure Subject Line">
    Set the email subject - this helps you identify automations in your inbox
    
    ```text
    Weekly Newsletter - Tech & AI
    ```
    
    <Tip>Use clear, searchable subjects so you can filter emails later</Tip>
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Write Your Query">
    Craft the task you want Khoj to perform. Be specific:
    
    **Good query:**
    ```text
    Compile a message including:
    1. A recap of AI news from the past week
    2. An at-home workout I can do in 20 minutes
    3. A quote to inspire me for the week ahead
    4. Summary of any notes I tagged #important this week
    ```
    
    **Too vague:**
    ```text
    Send me updates
    ```
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Set Schedule">
    Choose when and how often the automation runs:
    
    <Tabs>
      <Tab title="Quick Presets">
        - Daily at [time]
        - Weekly on [day] at [time]
        - Monthly on [date] at [time]
      </Tab>
      
      <Tab title="Custom Cron">
        Use cron syntax for advanced scheduling:
        
        ```
        0 9 * * 1        # Every Monday at 9 AM
        0 7 * * 1-5      # Weekdays at 7 AM
        0 18 1 * *       # 1st of month at 6 PM
        0 */6 * * *      # Every 6 hours
        ```
        
        [Cron syntax reference →](https://crontab.guru/)
      </Tab>
    </Tabs>
    
    <Note>
      All times use your local timezone (detected automatically)
    </Note>
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Choose Agent (Optional)">
    Select a specific agent to run the automation, or use the default assistant
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Enable Tools (Optional)">
    Toggle capabilities for this automation:
    - **Online Search:** Include real-time web research
    - **Document Search:** Access your knowledge base
    - **Image Generation:** Include images in emails
    - **Code Execution:** Run calculations or analysis
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Save Automation">
    Click **"Create"** - Khoj will run at the next scheduled time
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Managing Automations

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="View All Automations">
    The [automations page](https://app.khoj.dev/automations) shows:
    
    - **Subject & Query:** What the automation does
    - **Schedule:** When it runs
    - **Next Run:** Countdown to next execution
    - **Status:** Active, paused, or failed
  </Tab>
  
  <Tab title="Edit Automation">
    Click any automation to modify:
    - Change query or subject
    - Update schedule
    - Toggle tools
    - Switch agents
    
    Changes apply to the next run.
  </Tab>
  
  <Tab title="Pause/Resume">
    Toggle the status switch to:
    - **Pause:** Stop running (preserves settings)
    - **Resume:** Restart on schedule
    
    <Tip>Pause automations during vacations or when testing</Tip>
  </Tab>
  
  <Tab title="Delete Automation">
    Click the delete icon and confirm
    
    <Warning>Deleted automations cannot be recovered</Warning>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Email Delivery

### What to Expect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Scheduled Execution">
    At the scheduled time, Khoj processes your query in the background
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Email Sent">
    Results are sent to your account email address within a few minutes
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Review Results">
    The email contains:
    - Subject line you configured
    - Full response from Khoj
    - Any images or diagrams generated
    - Links to referenced documents
    - Footer with automation management link
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Email Formatting

Automation emails are formatted for readability:

- **Markdown rendered:** Headings, lists, and formatting preserved
- **Images embedded:** Generated images appear inline
- **Links active:** Citations and references are clickable
- **Mobile-friendly:** Readable on all devices

## Writing Effective Automation Queries

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Be Specific and Structured" icon="list-ol">
    Use numbered lists or sections:
    
    **Good:**
    ```text
    Create a weekly summary with:
    1. Top 3 news stories in renewable energy
    2. Summary of my journal entries this week
    3. Recommended article to read based on my interests
    4. Inspirational quote
    ```
    
    **Less effective:**
    ```text
    Give me a weekly summary of stuff
    ```
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Include Time References" icon="clock">
    Specify relevant time periods:
    
    ```text
    Summarize news from the past 7 days...
    ```
    
    ```text
    Based on my notes from yesterday...
    ```
    
    ```text
    What are this week's priorities from my calendar?
    ```
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Leverage Your Knowledge Base" icon="database">
    Reference your documents:
    
    ```text
    Review notes tagged #work from the past week and 
    highlight action items
    ```
    
    ```text
    Based on my reading list, recommend which book to 
    start next and why
    ```
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Request Actionable Output" icon="bullseye">
    Ask for practical results:
    
    ```text
    End with 3 specific actions I should take this week
    ```
    
    ```text
    Format as a checklist I can copy to my task manager
    ```
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Example Automations

<CodeGroup>
```text Daily Standup
Subject: Daily Standup Prep

Query:
Based on my notes and calendar:
1. What did I accomplish yesterday?
2. What's on my schedule today?
3. Any blockers or issues I noted?
4. Top 3 priorities for today

Schedule: Every weekday at 8:00 AM
```

```text Market Research
Subject: Competitor Intelligence Digest

Query:
Research the following companies: [Company A, Company B, Company C]

1. Recent product launches or announcements
2. News mentions or press releases
3. Hiring trends (are they expanding?)
4. Social media sentiment
5. Key takeaways for our strategy

Schedule: Every Friday at 4:00 PM
```

```text Content Ideas
Subject: Weekly Content Inspiration

Query:
Generate 5 blog post ideas for [your topic] based on:
1. Trending topics this week
2. Questions people are asking online
3. Gaps in existing content

For each idea, include:
- Catchy headline
- Target audience
- Key points to cover
- Estimated reading time

Schedule: Every Monday at 10:00 AM
```

```text Learning Path
Subject: This Week in Learning

Query:
Based on my goal to learn [topic]:

1. Recap what I studied in my notes last week
2. Suggest the next concept to learn
3. Provide a 20-minute learning exercise
4. Recommend one resource (article/video)
5. Quiz me with 3 questions on last week's topic

Schedule: Every Sunday at 7:00 PM
```
</CodeGroup>

## Self-Hosting Requirements

<Warning>
  Self-hosted Khoj requires additional setup for automations:
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Configure Authentication">
    Automations require user authentication to be enabled.
    
    [Authentication Setup Guide →](/api/authentication)
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Setup Email Service">
    Configure Resend for email delivery:
    
    1. Create account at [resend.com](https://resend.com)
    2. Generate API key
    3. Set environment variable:
       ```bash
       RESEND_API_KEY=your_api_key_here
       ```
    4. Configure sender email in Khoj settings
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Restart Server">
    Restart Khoj to apply email configuration
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Verify Setup">
    Create a test automation to confirm email delivery
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Cloud Khoj users ([app.khoj.dev](https://app.khoj.dev)) have automations fully configured - no setup needed.
</Note>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Not receiving emails">
    **Possible causes:**
    - Email in spam folder
    - Self-hosting without Resend configured
    - Incorrect email address in account settings
    
    **Solutions:**
    - Check spam/junk folders
    - Whitelist noreply@khoj.dev
    - Verify account email in settings
    - Check Resend configuration (self-hosted)
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Automation didn't run">
    **Possible causes:**
    - Automation paused
    - Server downtime during scheduled time
    - Timezone confusion
    
    **Solutions:**
    - Check automation status (active vs paused)
    - Verify schedule in your local timezone
    - Check "Last Run" timestamp
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Results are empty or generic">
    **Possible causes:**
    - Query too vague
    - Required tools not enabled
    - No relevant documents in knowledge base
    
    **Solutions:**
    - Make query more specific
    - Enable online search if needed
    - Verify documents are synced
    - Test query manually in chat first
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Emails too long">
    **Solutions:**
    - Ask for summaries or bullet points
    - Limit number of items requested
    - Be more specific about scope
    
    **Example:**
    ```text
    Summarize in 5 bullet points or less...
    ```
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
